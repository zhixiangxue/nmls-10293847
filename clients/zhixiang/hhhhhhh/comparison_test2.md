# DSCR Mortgage Guidelines Comparison Report

## Section 1: Delta Summary

This analysis compares **three DSCR loan programs** across approximately **45 distinct parameters** spanning credit requirements, leverage limits, property eligibility, investor qualifications, and program-specific features. The documents represent offerings from Platinum/Premier (Blue Point Mtg, Jan 2025), Angel Oak (April 2023), and Deephaven (Sept 2024).

**Top 5 Most Significant Differences Influencing Lending Decisions:**

1. **Maximum Loan Amount Disparity**: Deephaven permits loans up to **$2.5MM** (pg. 1) compared to Platinum's $1.5MM cap (pg. 1) and Angel Oak's $2MM limit (pg. 1). This 67% differential fundamentally changes the addressable market for high-value properties.

2. **Short-Term Rental (STR) Policy Divergence**: Angel Oak explicitly permits STR/Airbnb income with documented 12-month history on cash-out refinances (requires min 1.25 DSCR, 700 FICO, 70% LTV; pg. 1), while Platinum categorically states STR is "**Not Available**" (pg. 1). Deephaven is silent on STR policies. This represents a complete market segment exclusion for Platinum borrowers.

3. **Foreign National Access**: Both Angel Oak and Deephaven accommodate Foreign Nationals with specific overlays (70% LTV, 1.0 DSCR minimum; Angel Oak pg. 1, Deephaven pg. 1), whereas Platinum explicitly marks Foreign Nationals as "**Not Eligible**" (pg. 2). This eliminates international investor participation under Platinum.

4. **Credit Event Seasoning Requirements**: Platinum requires **48 months** from BK/FC/SS/DIL events (pg. 1), while both Angel Oak and Deephaven require only **36 months** (Angel Oak pg. 1, Deephaven pg. 1). This 12-month difference significantly impacts borrowers recovering from financial distress.

5. **Reserve Requirements Variance**: Platinum offers **zero reserves** at ≤70% LTV on purchase/rate-term (pg. 2), Angel Oak universally requires **6 months** (pg. 1), and Deephaven uses a tiered structure of **3-6 months** based on loan size (pg. 1). This creates substantial liquidity requirement differences ranging from $0 to potentially $20K+ in seasoned funds.

**Competitive Advantage by Category:**

- **Most Lenient Credit/FICO**: Deephaven (660 FICO eligible, pg. 1)
- **Highest Leverage**: Deephaven (80% LTV on purchase, pg. 1)  
- **Lowest Reserves**: Platinum (0 months at ≤70% LTV, pg. 2)
- **STR Accommodation**: Angel Oak (only program permitting STR, pg. 1)
- **Loan Size**: Deephaven ($2.5MM max, pg. 1)
- **Investor Access**: Angel Oak & Deephaven (Foreign National programs)

**Critical Parameter Gaps:**

- **DTI/Bank Statement Programs**: None of the three documents specify traditional DTI limits or alternative income documentation via bank statements
- **Geographic Restrictions**: Only Deephaven mentions Hawaii lava zone restrictions (pg. 1); other state-specific overlays are largely absent
- **Manufactured Housing**: All three are silent on manufactured home eligibility
- **Maximum Financed Properties**: Only Angel Oak specifies a 5-property/$3MM aggregate limit (pg. 1)
- **Entity Vesting Details**: While all reference entities, specific LLC/trust/corporation structuring requirements lack granular comparison data
- **Acreage Limits**: Only Deephaven references rural property with LTV reductions (pg. 1); specific acreage caps are not stated

The comparison reveals Deephaven as the most aggressive on leverage and loan limits, Angel Oak as the most accommodating for alternative investor classes (STR, Foreign Nationals), and Platinum as the most streamlined for low-reserve qualifying scenarios but with significant categorical exclusions.
